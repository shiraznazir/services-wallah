Our Installation Process

  • Consultation and Assessment :Our experts will visit your location to understand your needs and assess the space for optimal cooling solutions.
  • System Selection : Based on your requirements and budget, we guide you in selecting the most suitable air conditioning system.
  • Professional Installation : Our certified technicians ensure a precise and efficient installation, adhering to industry standards and safety protocols.
  • System Testing : After installation, we rigorously test the system to ensure it operates at peak performance.
  • Post-Installation Support : We provide ongoing support and maintenance to ensure your air conditioning system remains in excellent condition.

Our Maintenance Services

  • Routine Inspection : Regular checks to assess the performance and condition of your AC units.
  • Filter Cleaning and Replacement : Cleaning or replacing air filters to improve air quality and ensure smooth airflow.
  • Condenser and Evaporator Coil Maintenance : Removing dirt and debris from coils to enhance cooling efficiency.
  • Refrigerant Check and Recharge : Ensuring the refrigerant levels are optimal and addressing any leaks.
  • Electrical Component Check : Inspecting wiring, connections, and controls to prevent malfunctions.
  • Drain Line Cleaning : Clearing clogged drain lines to prevent water leakage and mold growth.

Air Conditioner Repair Services

  • Cooling Problems : Resolving inadequate cooling, uneven temperature distribution, and refrigerant leaks.
  • Noisy Units : Fixing unusual noises caused by fan, compressor, or duct issues.
  • Electrical Failures : Troubleshooting and repairing wiring, thermostat, and circuit problems.
  • Airflow Issues : Cleaning and repairing blocked filters, ducts, or coils for better air circulation.
  • Water Leakage : Addressing drainage and condensation problems to prevent damage and discomfort.
